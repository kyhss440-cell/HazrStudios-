🌿 Rest & Repair

Recovery, Stretching & Muscular Longevity

Movement creates the stimulus, but rest builds the adaptation. Follow these guidelines post-workout:

🫁
Down-Regulate Nervous System

Spend 3–5 minutes doing box breathing (4s in, 4s hold, 4s out, 4s hold) immediately after high intensity to flip from sympathetic to parasympathetic repair mode.

💧
Electrolyte & Water Rehydration

Drink 500ml of water with a pinch of unrefined sea salt or coconut water to replenish cellular sodium and potassium lost during sweating.

🧘
Static Hip & Thoracic Releases

Hold static stretches (hip flexors, hamstrings, pecs) for 45–60 seconds after your workout when muscles are warm, never before explosive movements.

🌙
Deep Sleep & Growth Hormone

Over 90% of exercise-induced human growth hormone (HGH) secretion occurs during stage 3 and 4 slow-wave sleep. Prioritize a cool, dark room.

The 4 Pillars of Balanced Eating

Protein provides essential amino acids needed for muscle synthesis, immune defense, and satiety hormones like peptide YY. Prioritize lean poultry, wild salmon, eggs, tofu, tempeh, and Greek yogurt.

Tip: Aim for 25–35g per main meal to trigger muscle protein synthesis.

Complex carbs (sweet potatoes, oats, quinoa, berries, legumes) deliver sustained glucose release without sharp insulin spikes. Dietary fiber fuels beneficial gut microbiota and supports cholesterol regulation.

Tip: Aim for at least 28–35g of total dietary fiber per day.

Monounsaturated and omega-3 polyunsaturated fats (extra virgin olive oil, avocados, chia seeds, walnuts) protect cardiovascular health, facilitate fat-soluble vitamin absorption (A, D, E, K), and regulate cell signaling.

  • Palm: 1 portion of protein (~20-30g)
  • Fist: 1 portion of dense vegetables
  • Cupped Hand: 1 portion of carbohydrate foods
  • Thumb: 1 portion of healthy dietary fats / oils

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how NutriFit operates on your device.

NutriFit utilizes HTML5 browser localStorage, an encrypted, isolated sandbox built directly into Google Chrome and modern browsers. Your data never touches a remote server or third-party database. You can export a full .json backup anytime via the Preferences menu.

Our nutrition calculator utilizes the scientifically validated Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the gold standard used by registered dietitians globally, factoring in biological sex, age, height, weight, activity multipliers, and sustainable goal targets.

NutriFit Guide is a 100% hardcoded, rule-based wellness companion written entirely in vanilla JavaScript. It does not use any external AI APIs, external servers, or API keys. It matches keywords and synonyms locally to recommend recipes, workouts, water reminders, and website guidance.

Click on the gear icon or "User Preferences" in the footer, switch to the "Data Backup & Reset" tab, and click "Export Backup JSON". You can then import that file onto any browser or device instantly.
